# PowerPoint Masked Image Overlay

![Screenshot of the prompt in use](./assets/demo.png)

## Summary

This prompt helps create a premium editorial poster slide in PowerPoint where a person becomes a stencil-shaped window cut through a flat white panel, revealing the background image underneath.

## Prompt

```text
Create a premium editorial poster composition with a business professional looking slightly above as the central graphic device.
Overlay existing background with a separate layer on large flat rectangular panel in a white solid color.
Transform person into a bold stencil-shaped cutout through the foreground panel. The background image must remain visible inside the cutout, creating the effect that the subject has been physically removed from the solid panel.
Scale the cutout dramatically and crop portions of it against the panel boundaries. Let selected parts of the silhouette break through or extend toward the outer background to create depth and visual tension.
Keep the foreground panel completely flat and minimal. Keep the cutout organic and expressive but instantly recognizable as person. Use crisp masking, clean edges, strong negative space, sophisticated asymmetry, and an art-directed magazine/poster aesthetic.
Do not render the subject as a separate object sitting on top of the panel. The subject must function as a transparent window revealing the background underneath.
```

## Description

This prompt is designed for Copilot in PowerPoint when a slide has a background image. The result is a masked image overlay: the subject's silhouette acts as a transparent window in a solid panel, making the background appear through the person.

## Use cases

- Title slides and section openers with a bold editorial look.
- Event, conference, or keynote posters.
- Monthly business reviews and executive briefings.

## Instructions

1. Open PowerPoint.
2. Add a background image to a slide.
3. Open Copilot in PowerPoint.
4. Paste the prompt from the Prompt section.
5. Review the generated slide, then add or adjust title text in the white panel.

## Prerequisites

- Copilot in PowerPoint.

## Help

This sample is provided for community use and learning. If the subject appears as an object on top of the panel instead of a window, re-run the prompt and emphasize that the background must show through the silhouette.
